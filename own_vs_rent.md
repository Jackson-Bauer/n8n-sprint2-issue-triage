Own vs rent comparison

In terms of reproducing the first two nodes in Zapier compared to n8n, I believe the time differences were negligible. They both took roughly the same amount of time, but that was without using the ai building features on both. When it comes to scalability, I think for most cases n8n will be the best option. the biggest limitation will be sharing the workflow, which is something a rented platform makes easier. N8n is self hosted, so setting it up to work on multiple machines will be much more complicated than a renting platform like Zapier. 

For this specific workflow in sprint 2, I believe n8n is the better choice. The biggest factor is looking at Zapier pricing (accessed October 11 2026), which showed me something very interesting. On Zapier, the $20 Professional plan includes webhooks. I noticed that the free plan did not, and so I assume this workflow would not even work on Zapier. The fact that you have to pay to even recreate the workflow from n8n makes me leans towards n8n.

For scalability and amount of uses, I think n8n will be the best option for most cases. Small and even some medium sized companies shouldn’t encounter bottlenecks in n8n, as long as the hosted machine is powerful enough. Over the long term, it might even be better to purchase a very powerful machine that can host large quantities of requests in n8n, but for the short term renting through Zapier could be better. The monthly cost for a larger company would obviously be lower than buying a sufficient machine to self host, but it is up to them to compare how many months of renting are necessary before owning becomes more cost effective.

Overall, I think most cases have n8n and owning as the superior method. There are obvious benefits for using Zapier and renting, mainly with very large scales or a lack of powerful hardware, but I think those only account for a small percentage of cases. If the average user or group asked me which they should use, I would feel confident in suggesting they own.
